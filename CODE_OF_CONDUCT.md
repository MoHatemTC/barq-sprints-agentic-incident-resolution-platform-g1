# Code of conduct

This is a Sprints virtual internship team delivering a real project for BARQ Systems.
Five people depend on each other's work landing, so how we treat each other is part of
the deliverable.

## What we expect

**Help each other.** The team's outcome is the outcome. Ask, offer, unblock.

**Ask questions early.** There are no bad ones. Silence costs the team more than any
question does.

**Critique the work, not the person.** "Our handoffs were messy" is useful. "X was
slow" is not. This holds in reviews, stand-ups and retrospectives.

**Say when you are stuck.** Being blocked is normal. Hiding it until the deadline is
the one thing that actually damages the sprint.

**Own mistakes plainly.** State what happened, what it affects, and what you are doing
about it. No one is judged for a mistake that was reported and fixed.

## In code review

Review against the requirement, not personal preference. When you disagree, say why
and point at the requirement, the code, or an executed test — not at seniority.

A review that blocks a merge must say what would unblock it.

## Not acceptable

Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or comments about someone's identity
rather than their work. Taking credit for another person's contribution, or rewriting
history to obscure who did what.

## Raising a problem

Talk to the Scrum Master first. If that is not appropriate, contact your mentor, or
the internship group admins through the official programme channel.
